TOP SECRET

Be on court the second it opens.

Your club releases courts to the second, exactly four days ahead, and the best evening slots are gone before most people have logged in. Court Sniper wakes your phone, waits for the release moment and locks on a court for you. No alarms, no refreshing, no fumbling with the website.

How it works Get access

The operation

  1. Brief a mission. Pick the date, start time, length and the courts you will accept, in order of preference.
  2. Stand down. The app schedules a wake-up shortly before the release moment, even if your phone is asleep.
  3. Lock on. It starts trying 15 seconds early and keeps going, matching the server clock to the second, dropping any court someone else grabs and moving to your next choice.
  4. Target acquired. You get a notification with the court and time. Cancel any booking from the Assets tab.

Field kit

Release-second timing

Syncs to the booking server's clock so the first attempt lands at the exact release second, not a moment late.

Court fallbacks

Rank your courts. If Court 1 is taken the instant it opens, it tries Court 2, then 3, without you lifting a finger.

Intel

See which courts are free for every start time over the next few days. Slots already in the past are hidden.

Assets

Your upcoming bookings in one place, with one-tap cancel when plans change.

Password manager

Sign in with Google Password Manager, or save your details there. Credentials on the device are sealed in the Android Keystore.

Noir or Paper

Black and gold spy-thriller styling, or a cream dossier with typewriter type. Your choice is remembered.
