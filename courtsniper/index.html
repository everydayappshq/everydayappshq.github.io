The operation

Four steps. Zero stress.

  1. BriefPick the date, start time, length and the courts you will accept, in order of preference.
  2. Stand downThe app schedules a wake-up shortly before release, even if your phone is asleep in your pocket.
  3. Lock onIt starts 15 seconds early, syncs to the server clock, keeps firing, and drops any court someone else grabs.
  4. AcquiredA notification tells you the court and time. Cancel from the Assets tab if plans change.

Field kit

Everything an agent needs.

ITEM 01

Release-second timing

Matches the booking server's clock so the first shot lands on the exact release second, not a moment late.

ITEM 02

Court fallbacks

Rank your courts. If Court 1 is gone the instant it opens, it tries Court 2, then 3, without you lifting a finger.

ITEM 03

Intel

See which courts are free at every start time over the coming days. Slots already in the past are hidden.

ITEM 04

Assets

Your upcoming bookings in one place, with one-tap cancel.

ITEM 05

Password manager

Sign in with Google Password Manager or save your details there. On-device credentials are sealed in the Android Keystore.

ITEM 06

Noir or Paper

Black and gold spy-thriller styling, or a cream dossier in typewriter type. It remembers your choice.
